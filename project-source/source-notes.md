# Historical and devotional sources

Research checked 31 October 2026. The website’s chapter summaries and mystery reflections are original paraphrases. Traditional English prayer texts are historic devotional forms; “Holy Ghost” is the traditional English equivalent of “Holy Spirit.” Optional local customs are not represented as universally mandatory.

- [John Paul II, Rosarium Virginis Mariae (16 October 2002)](https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/apost_letters/2002/documents/hf_jp-ii_apl_20021016_rosarium-virginis-mariae.html), especially §§1, 19–21, 40–43: Christ-centered devotion and the Luminous Mysteries.
- [Paul VI, Marialis Cultus (2 February 1974)](https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/en/apost_exhortations/documents/hf_p-vi_exh_19740202_marialis-cultus.html), §§42–55: scriptural and contemplative character, 150 Hail Marys and the Psalter, relationship to liturgy.
- [Leo XIII, Supremi Apostolatus Officio (1 September 1883)](https://www.vatican.va/content/leo-xiii/en/encyclicals/documents/hf_l-xiii_enc_01091883_supremi-apostolatus-officio.html), §§3–4, 8: Catholic commemoration of Lepanto and October observance. Its devotional assertions about Dominic are not independent documentary proof of a thirteenth-century apparition.
- [Order of Preachers, Confraternity of the Most Holy Rosary](https://www.op.org/confraternity-of-the-most-holy-rosary/): Alan de la Roche, Jacob Sprenger, Cologne 1475. The website avoids contested exact dates for Alan’s earliest confraternity.
- [English Dominicans, What is the Rosary?](https://english.op.org/about-us/rosary/what-is-the-rosary/): Pius V and Consueverunt Romani Pontifices, 1569.
- [Catholic Encyclopedia, The Rosary](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/13184b.htm): historical critique of origins, medieval prayer counters and Paternosters. This is an older Catholic reference, used alongside current Dominican and papal sources.
- [Catholic Encyclopedia, Dominic of Prussia](https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05112b.htm): Carthusian meditative clauses. Dominic of Prussia is not St. Dominic, founder of the Dominicans.
- [Shrine of Fátima, Narrative of the Apparitions](https://www.fatima.pt/en/pages/narrative-of-the-apparitions-): accounts of Lúcia, Francisco, and Jacinta and the requests for Rosary prayer. Reconstructed visual scenes must be identified as artistic, not photographs of apparitions.
- [Catechism §67](https://www.usccb.org/sites/default/files/flipbooks/catechism/25/): private revelation does not form part of the deposit of faith.
- [USCCB, How to Pray the Rosary](https://www.usccb.org/how-to-pray-the-rosary): sequence, introductory prayers, Glory Be between decades, optional Fatima prayer, concluding prayers.
- [Vatican, Joyful Mysteries](https://www.vatican.va/special/rosary/documents/misteri_gaudiosi_en.html): biblical passages and traditional prayer forms. Additional mystery references appear in the text itself; Assumption and Coronation are explicitly distinguished from directly narrated Gospel episodes.
- [Vatican News, Worldwide Rosary initiative (May 2021)](https://www.vaticannews.va/en/church/news/2021-05/shrines-around-the-world-linked-for-recitation-of-the-rosary.html): documented examples from Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas, and Oceania. These markers demonstrate later worldwide practice, not claims of where the Rosary originated.

Globe coordinates are rounded geographic navigation aids; Gulf of Patras and southern France are regional markers, not exact historical event coordinates. The map’s selection of communities is illustrative rather than exhaustive.

All historical scene images should be labeled artistic reconstructions. Source links identify factual support, not a license to copy source photographs or modern translations. No source photography is bundled by this content module.
