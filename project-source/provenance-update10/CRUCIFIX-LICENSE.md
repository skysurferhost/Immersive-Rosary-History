# Crucifix corpus — source and adaptation

Source: **Crucifix**, The Hunt Museum, accession HCM 066.
Record: https://zenodo.org/records/18948596
Original model: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/crucifix-9e5f9a61cf914e649dec44041affe1d2

License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The museum describes a rosewood crucifix with a boxwood corpus, ornate terminals, a crown of thorns, a short flowing loincloth, overlapping nailed feet and an INRI plaque. Attribution to Andrea Fantoini is qualified as probable by the museum; this site does not claim certain authorship.

Adapted for The Rosary Through Time: extracted the corpus from the scanned altar cross, removed detached scan fragments, closed cut boundaries, reduced polygon count, rescaled uniformly, and replaced surface materials with an antique-metal finish. The existing original project cross and INRI plaque are retained. The adaptation is a devotional digital rendering, not a faithful presentation of the museum object's original materials or dimensions.
